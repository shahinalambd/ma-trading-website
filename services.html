How it works

The same simple process for every service, from first message to payment.

  1. Share the details

    Send photos, material type and approximate quantity by phone or email.

  2. Get a price and schedule

    We assess the material or site and agree a fair price and a time that suits you.

  3. Collection or site work

    Our team arrives with the right equipment and handles loading, cutting or dismantling safely.

  4. Weighing and payment

    Material is weighed and documented, and you are paid as agreed.

Frequently asked questions

What types of scrap do you buy?

We buy aluminium, copper, wires and cables, brass, lead, nickel, zinc, stainless steel, ferrous scrap and mixed metals, as well as plastic, wood, paper and computer scrap.

How is the price of scrap decided?

The price depends on the material type, its grade and condition, the quantity and current market rates. Send photos and an approximate quantity and we will give you a price.

Do you collect scrap from our site?

Yes. Our team comes with the right equipment for loading, and material is weighed and documented.

Can you clear a complete site?

Yes. We handle dismantling, loading, transport and clean-up, so the site is left clear and safe.

How quickly can you respond?

We offer 24-hour telephone support and reply to every enquiry within 24 hours.

Have scrap to sell or a site to clear?

Send us the details and get a free quote within 24 hours.
